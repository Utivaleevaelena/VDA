I am an HMONP architect with more than fifteen years of experience in healthcare and research architecture, residential projects and renovation. My career combines work within architecture firms in France and Monaco with experience in independent practice.

I contributed in particular to the Princess Grace Hospital Centre with AIA Life Designers, then to the design studies and technical coordination of Le Schuylkill with Square Architecte, on a project carried out in collaboration with Zaha Hadid Architects.

Hospital architecture shapes how I look at living spaces: understanding how places are used, considering light and context, then linking architectural choices to people’s needs. In renovation, I aim to help buildings evolve without losing their identity.
